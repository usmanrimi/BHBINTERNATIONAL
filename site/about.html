Corporate Profile

Direct Fleet Control & Multi-Sector Capability

BHB International Ltd is a Nigerian company with a core focus on integrated haulage and logistics management through its company-owned fleet.

We also undertake general trading, importation and sales of truck heads and flatbed trailers, and contract-based execution of CSR, water and facility infrastructure projects across Nigeria.

Our transport operations cover loaded container movement from ports to inland destinations, customer-requested export commodity haulage, empty container positioning and returns, and dedicated road freight. By operating our own fleet of truck heads and trailers, we maintain direct control over dispatch, maintenance, scheduling and safety standards.

Beyond haulage, we deliver practical contracting solutions, from commercial supply and equipment sales to turnkey solar street lighting, school renovations, and borehole water installations.

Executive Profile

Zango Salihu Habibu is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of BHB International Ltd, a diversified Nigerian company specialising in integrated haulage and logistics management, general trading, procurement, contracting and project services.

An entrepreneur and business executive with professional experience spanning the oil and gas, financial services, transportation, logistics and contracting sectors, he brings a multidisciplinary approach to business leadership, operational management and commercial development.

Before establishing BHB International Ltd, he held various professional positions in the oil and gas industry, gaining experience in petroleum trading and supply operations, marine logistics, demurrage management, insurance and claims administration, and commercial operations. His subsequent experience in the financial services sector further strengthened his capabilities in business operations, customer relationship management and commercial coordination.

Since establishing BHB International Ltd in 2016, he has led the company's development and diversification, overseeing its company-owned haulage fleet, container transportation operations, freight logistics, commercial procurement and trading activities.

His business interests also extend to the importation and trading of heavy-duty vehicles and transportation equipment, general merchandise, and the coordination and execution of contract-based projects.

Through BHB International Ltd, he has been involved in various project-related activities, including marine and vessel operations, towage and mooring coordination, civil works, building renovations, solar street lighting, water infrastructure and community empowerment initiatives.

His leadership approach emphasises operational efficiency, accountability, strategic partnerships and sustainable business growth. He remains committed to strengthening BHB International Ltd's operational capabilities, expanding commercial opportunities and delivering reliable, value-driven solutions to clients across multiple sectors.

Our Commitment

Health, Safety, Quality & Environment (HSQE)

BHB International Ltd recognises the importance of health, safety, quality and environmental responsibility in transportation and project delivery. We are committed to promoting safe working practices, appropriate vehicle maintenance, operational risk awareness and compliance with applicable requirements. In project assignments, we emphasise responsible procurement, suitable technical expertise, effective supervision and quality-focused execution.

Occupational Health & Safety

Driver fatigue management, safe loading practices, appropriate PPE, and proactive journey management protocols.

Fleet Roadworthiness & Quality

Rigorous preventive vehicle maintenance schedules, brake and tyre checks, and adherence to haulage standards.

Environmental Care

Compliance & Supervision

Full statutory alignment with federal transport regulations, verified procurement, and disciplined project supervision.
