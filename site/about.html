About BHB International

Driving business. Delivering projects. Creating impact.

BHB International Ltd is a Nigerian-owned business built around entrepreneurship, strategic partnerships, project execution and dependable service delivery across key sectors.

Experience across transport, construction and the sea

BHB International Ltd is a Nigerian-owned business built around entrepreneurship, strategic partnerships, project execution and service delivery.

Our activities have grown across interconnected sectors, giving us a broad understanding of transportation, logistics, contracting, operations, infrastructure development and maritime services.

We work with private businesses, contractors, government institutions, agencies, communities and strategic partners to identify opportunities, mobilise resources and coordinate projects from concept through execution.
