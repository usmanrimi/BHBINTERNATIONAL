Client Segments

Industries & Client Segments

Our services are designed for importers, exporters, clearing and forwarding agents, manufacturers, construction and engineering companies, trading businesses, public institutions, corporate CSR teams and development organisations.

Cargo Owners & Exporters

Transportation of loaded import containers from port to inland depots, and timely movement of agricultural or solid mineral export commodities to ports.

Clearing & Forwarding Agents

Reliable truck availability for timely terminal evacuation, container haulage, and prompt empty container turnaround to shipping lines.

Manufacturers & Industrial Firms

Inbound raw material supply, industrial consumables haulage, and distribution coordination to regional warehouses across Nigeria.

Construction & Engineering

Flatbed trailer transport for heavy machinery, structural steel, pre-cast components, equipment and building project materials.

Commercial Trading Companies

Supply of general merchandise, consumables, and direct importation and sales of truck tractor heads and flatbed trailers.

Public Institutions & CSR Teams

Contract-based delivery of solar street lighting, school and healthcare facility renovations, and community borehole water projects.

Commodity Aggregators

Transit arrangements connecting farms and processing centres across northern Nigeria with coastal processing terminals and export corridors.

Forwarders & Warehousing Operators

Coordinated haulage between port berths, bonded terminals, inland dry ports and regional distribution warehouses.
