Principal Division 01

Integrated Transport & Logistics Services

Focused service lines designed to support cargo movement, company-owned fleet utilisation and end-to-end transport coordination.

01 / CORE SERVICE

Loaded Container & Export Commodity Haulage

Transportation of loaded containers from ports to inland destinations, and export commodities for customers from farms, warehouses and processing centres to ports or designated loading points, subject to availability and booking.

  • Loaded container transportation
  • Customer-requested export commodity haulage
  • Bulk and containerised commodities
  • Export cargo collection and port delivery
02 / CORE SERVICE

Container Haulage & Road Freight

Road transportation of loaded and empty containers, general freight and commercial cargo across designated routes.

  • Full-container transportation
  • Inland container movements
  • Empty container returns
  • Warehouse and terminal deliveries
03 / FLEET MANAGEMENT

Fleet & Transport Management

Management of our company-owned fleet, including transport planning, vehicle allocation, maintenance oversight, dispatch and delivery coordination.

  • Company-owned truck allocation
  • Driver and trip coordination
  • Fleet scheduling and preventive maintenance
  • Transport performance monitoring
04 / PORT OPERATIONS

Port & Inland Logistics

Coordinating road freight activities linked to seaports, terminals, inland depots and commercial destinations.

  • Terminal pickup coordination
  • Port-to-inland deliveries
  • Container positioning and returns
  • Coordination with forwarding agents
05 / HEAVY HAULAGE

Project & Specialised Cargo Logistics

Transport planning and arrangements for heavy machinery, construction materials, industrial equipment and project cargo, subject to equipment suitability and route requirements.

  • Equipment movement planning
  • Flatbed transport coordination
  • Project material deliveries
06 / WAREHOUSING

Warehousing & Distribution Coordination

Arrangements for cargo storage, consolidation, onward distribution and warehouse-to-destination movements through available facilities and partners.

  • Storage coordination
  • Cargo distribution planning
  • Warehouse dispatch logistics
07 / ASSET CONTROL

Company-Owned Fleet Operations

Direct operation and management of our own trucks to provide dependable, accountable road freight services. By maintaining our own tractors and flatbeds, we eliminate middleman uncertainties and ensure complete transit transparency.

Company-Owned Deployment

Dedicated truck allocation for scheduled trips and volume clients.

Driver & Dispatch Oversight

Vetted drivers trained in safe driving and journey management.

Preventive Maintenance

Routine technical checkups to ensure high uptime and safe transit.

End-to-End Oversight

Proactive status updates from origin departure to proof of delivery.

Flexible Backhaul Opportunities

More Than Empty Container Returns

Where customer demand and truck availability align, we transport export commodities and other approved cargo on suitable routes, creating productive return journeys alongside our regular container haulage operations.

Principal Division 02

General Trading, Contracting & Project Services

Beyond haulage, BHB International undertakes commercial supply, project-based contracts and community infrastructure assignments tailored to client requirements.

01 / TRADING

General Trading & Merchandise

Buying, selling and supplying general merchandise, consumables, equipment and other goods for commercial and institutional customers.

  • Commercial merchandise supply
  • Consumables and equipment procurement
  • Institutional supply contracts
02 / EQUIPMENT

Truck Heads & Flatbed Trailers

Importation, purchase and sale of truck tractor heads, flatbed trailers and related transport equipment. This trading activity is distinct from our company-owned haulage fleet.

  • Direct importation of truck tractor heads
  • Flatbed trailer supply and sales
  • Heavy-duty commercial vehicle procurement
03 / EMPOWERMENT

CSR & Empowerment Programmes

Contract-based planning, procurement, coordination and implementation of skills acquisition, livelihood support and community empowerment programmes for corporate and institutional clients.

  • Vocational equipment procurement
  • Community empowerment delivery
  • Socio-economic impact initiatives
04 / ENERGY

Solar Street Lighting

Project coordination, supply and installation arrangements for solar-powered street lighting and related community electrification initiatives.

  • Solar-powered street lighting supply
  • Installation planning and coordination
  • Community lighting infrastructure
05 / RENOVATION

School & Facility Renovation

Renovation, rehabilitation and upgrading of schools, classrooms and community facilities, including civil and related improvement works.

  • Educational facility rehabilitation
  • Classroom upgrade and civil repairs
  • Community building renovations
06 / WATER INFRASTRUCTURE

Boreholes & Water Treatment

Borehole development, water supply infrastructure and water treatment project coordination to improve access to dependable water services.

  • Borehole drilling and pump installation
  • Water reticulation and storage setups
  • Community water facility delivery
