Habibu Zango

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

“Sustainable business success is built on integrity, professionalism, effective resource management and long-term relationships founded on trust and mutual value.”

Executive Profile

Habibu Zango is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of BHB International Ltd, a diversified Nigerian company specialising in integrated haulage and logistics management, general trading, procurement, contracting and project services.

An entrepreneur and business executive with professional experience spanning the oil and gas, financial services, transportation, logistics and contracting sectors, he brings a multidisciplinary approach to business leadership, operational management and commercial development.

Before establishing BHB International Ltd in 2016, he held various professional positions in the oil and gas industry, gaining experience in petroleum trading and supply operations, marine logistics, demurrage management, insurance and claims administration, and commercial operations. His subsequent experience in the financial services sector further strengthened his capabilities in business operations, customer relationship management and commercial coordination.

Since establishing BHB International Ltd, he has led the company's development and diversification, overseeing its company-owned haulage fleet, container transportation operations, freight logistics, commercial procurement and trading activities.

His leadership approach emphasises operational efficiency, accountability, strategic partnerships and sustainable business growth across Nigeria.
