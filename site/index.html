About our company

One Logistics Partner.
Coordinated Solutions.

BHB International Ltd is a Nigerian company with a core focus on integrated haulage and logistics management through its company-owned fleet.

We also undertake general trading, importation and sales of truck heads and flatbed trailers, and contract-based execution of CSR, water and facility infrastructure projects across Nigeria.

Our operations are built on direct fleet control, structured dispatch, clear route planning and disciplined execution from enquiry to final delivery.

  • Customer-requested export commodity haulage
  • Bulk and containerised commodities
  • Export cargo collection and port delivery
02 / Core Service

Container Haulage & Road Freight

Road transportation of loaded and empty containers, general freight and commercial cargo across designated routes.

  • Full-container transportation

Management of our company-owned fleet, including transport planning, vehicle allocation, maintenance oversight, dispatch and delivery coordination.

Port & Inland Logistics

Coordinating road freight activities linked to seaports, terminals, inland depots and commercial destinations.

  • Terminal pickup coordination

Project & Specialised Cargo Logistics

Transport planning and arrangements for heavy machinery, construction materials, industrial equipment and project cargo, subject to equipment suitability and route requirements.

Executive Profile

Meet Our Founder & CEO

Zango Salihu Habibu is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of BHB International Ltd, a diversified Nigerian company specialising in integrated haulage and logistics management, general trading, procurement, contracting and project services.

An entrepreneur and business executive with professional experience spanning the oil and gas, financial services, transportation, logistics and contracting sectors, he brings a multidisciplinary approach to business leadership, operational management and commercial development.

Since establishing BHB International Ltd in 2016, he has led the company's development and diversification, overseeing its company-owned haulage fleet, container transportation operations, freight logistics, commercial procurement and trading activities.

His leadership approach emphasises operational efficiency, accountability, strategic partnerships and sustainable business growth. Under his direction, BHB International Ltd delivers reliable, value-driven solutions across Nigeria.
