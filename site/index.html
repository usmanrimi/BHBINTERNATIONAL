Executive Leadership

Habibu Zango is an entrepreneur, business executive and project-development professional with multidisciplinary experience across haulage and transportation, logistics, project management, infrastructure development, marine operations, contracting and business development.

As Chief Executive Officer of BHB International Ltd, he has been involved in container haulage, transportation and logistics operations since 2016. His earlier experience includes operations and marketing in the financial-services sector, and extensive work in contracting, project coordination and development initiatives.

Through this work he has coordinated human development and empowerment programmes, civil construction, building renovation, electrical works, solar street-lighting projects, and borehole and water infrastructure for communities and institutions.

He also has substantial experience in marine and maritime business development, including towage operations, vessel management, shipping services and dredging. He engages government agencies, institutions, contractors and private-sector stakeholders to develop marine-service opportunities and long-term contracts.
